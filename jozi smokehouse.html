The Story

Built For Jozi Food Lovers.

Rooted directly in the vibrant city streets at 264 Fox Street, Maboneng, Jozi SmokeHouse is where premium slow-cooked technique crashes into raw Johannesburg street culture.

We are completely obsessed with rich woodsmoke flavor, top-tier cuts, and the unmatched energy of our local food community. Every wing glaze, every sauce drip, and every char line is crafted to offer you a premium, authentic street-gourmet standard.

"Where food meets soul."
